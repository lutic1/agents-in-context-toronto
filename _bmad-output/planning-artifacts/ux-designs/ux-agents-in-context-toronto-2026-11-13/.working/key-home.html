Tumour-informed ctDNA MRD (approach)

Assay development

A new study builds a tumour-informed ctDNA test for each patient after NSCLC surgery.

This study builds a patient-specific, tumor-informed MRD panel for each participant, instead of relying on a fixed generic gene panel, and evaluates plasma and BWF MRD in parallel.
